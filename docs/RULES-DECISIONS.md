# Rules decisions

Where the engine had to interpret or simplify a rule, it is written down here so the owner can overrule it. Newest entries go at the bottom of each section.

## Simplifications (to be removed)

| #   | Area                  | What the engine does now                                                                                                                                                                           | Planned                                     |
| --- | --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| S6  | Targets per weapon    | All of a weapon's attacks go to one target unit; attacks of one weapon cannot be split between targets.                                                                                            | When the UI allocates per model.            |
| S7  | Automatic choices     | The engine picks the critical-hit ability of a weapon with several (best expected damage), spends rally points (models first, then healing) and sets up returned models.                           | Player choice if the owner wants it.        |
| S8  | Weapon choices        | Every model attacks with every weapon on its warscroll unless the army list entry gives a loadout (`weapons`). The placeholder Seraphon list has none, so its Kroxigor swing both hammer and maul. | Loadouts on every list entry with a choice. |
| S9  | Incoherent casualties | Casualties are picked to keep the unit coherent. If no choice does, extra models are removed at once until it is coherent (the rule does this at the end of the turn).                             | Revisit if it ever matters in play.         |
| S13 | Manifestation parts   | Summoned manifestations use their single resolved warscroll footprint and statline; warscroll passive/activated abilities on manifestations come in M4.                                            | M4.                                         |

## Interpretations

| #   | Rule                            | Reading used                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| --- | ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| I1  | Objective range                 | A model contests an objective when its base is within 3" of the 40mm marker, i.e. within 3.79" of the objective's centre.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| I2  | Terrain types                   | Authored layout for Into the Fire: ruins give cover and are unstable; outcrops give cover and are obscuring; the two spires are the Places of Power and are obscuring.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| I3  | Priority                        | Round 1: player 0 (the attacker) goes first. From round 2 both roll a D6; the winner picks first or second; a tie goes to the player who went first last round (no re-roll).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| I4  | Seizing, underdog               | Going second last round, winning the roll and going first seizes the initiative unless the opponent leads by 11+ VP. The underdog is the last seizer's opponent, else the player with fewer VP, else nobody (ported from aos-webapp).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| I5  | Command points                  | 4 CP each at the start of every round, +1 for the underdog, after priority is decided. Unused CP are lost at the end of the round.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| I6  | Critical hits                   | An unmodified hit roll of 6 is a critical hit only for a weapon with a Crit ability. On any other weapon a 6 is an ordinary roll: modifiers apply and it has no special effect. **Confirmed by the owner, 2026-11-10.**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| I7  | Modifier caps                   | Hit and wound modifiers total at most +1 and at least -1; save modifiers at most +1 with no floor. Rend and damage modifiers are not capped. A unit with no save (-) makes no save rolls, even with +1.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| I8  | Attacks                         | Range is measured base edge to base edge from each model; only models in range of the target attack (melee: within 3"). A variable Attacks value is rolled per model. A champion adds 1 attack only if it is one of the attacking models.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| I9  | Weapon abilities                | Anti-X is cumulative across abilities; Anti-charge means the target charged this turn. Charge (+n Damage) needs the attacker to have charged. Companion weapons ignore friendly positive modifiers except All-out Attack and their own abilities.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| I10 | Damage and wards                | All of a unit's attacks are rolled before any damage is allocated. Wards are rolled per damage point (mortal damage included). Damage beyond a slain model carries to the next; the pool is cleared when the unit is destroyed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| I11 | Casualties                      | The engine picks: non-champions first, then the model furthest from any enemy, never breaking coherency when a choice keeps it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| I12 | Shooting in combat              | A unit in combat can shoot only with Shoot in Combat weapons, at any enemy in range.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| I13 | Charges                         | Declared by a unit not in combat that did not run or retreat, and only offered with an enemy within 12.5". 2D6; it can succeed if an enemy unit is within the roll + 1/2". Each model moves up to the roll; one model must end within 1/2".                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| I14 | Fighting                        | Players alternate picks from the active player; a player with an eligible unit must pick. Pile-in: each model up to 3", ending no further from the nearest enemy model than it started. A charged unit with no enemy in range still "fights".                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| I15 | Retreat                         | The D3 mortal damage (warded) is suffered once the unit is placed; cancelling the retreat costs nothing.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| I16 | Objective control               | A model contests only its nearest objective in range. Control score is the sum of the Control of contesting models; a higher score takes control, a tie keeps it. Control is resolved and scored at the end of every turn, round 1 included.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| I17 | Rally                           | Any hero phase (yours or the enemy's); offered only to a unit not in combat with damage or slain models. Obeys the core command limits (1 command per unit per phase, each command once per army per phase). Returned models are set up touching a model of the unit, coherent and more than 3" from enemy units.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| I18 | At the Double                   | Declared as a run whose roll counts as 6 (the place decision shows a die of 6). Commands are used once per unit and once per army in each phase.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| I19 | All-out Attack                  | +1 to hit for that unit's attacks, Companion weapons included. No save penalty. **Confirmed by the owner, 2026-11-10** (the earlier engine also subtracted 1 from the unit's save rolls; that was wrong).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| I20 | All-out Defence                 | Offered to the defender when their unit is the target of declared attacks and they have the CP; +1 to save for those attacks only.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| I21 | Magic & casting                 | Wizards use spell, summon, unbind and banish abilities up to their power level (`UnitProfile.wizard`) per phase. Casting rolls 2D6; an unmodified double 1 is a miscast (the spell fails, the caster suffers D3 mortal damage and cannot cast more spells that phase). Non-`UNLIMITED` spells/prayers are once per turn per army; `UNLIMITED` spells/prayers are once per turn per unit.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| I22 | Unbind                          | When an enemy spell or summon meets its casting value, any friendly Wizard within 30" of the caster with remaining power level may react with `Unbind` (spending 1 power level for the phase) and roll 2D6; if the roll exceeds the casting total, the spell is unbound.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| I23 | Prayers & chanting              | Priests use prayer and banish abilities up to their power level (`UnitProfile.priest`) per phase. Chanting rolls 1D6: an unmodified 1 loses D3 ritual points (down to 0); on 2+, the roll is added to `ritualPoints` and either banked (if below the chanting value or chosen) or spent to answer the prayer (resetting `ritualPoints` to 0).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| I24 | Manifestations                  | Summon spells set up the manifestation wholly within the summon range of the caster and (except Chronomantic Cogs and Soulsnare Shackles) > 9" from enemies. Manifestations cannot move in the movement phase of the turn they are set up, do not contest objectives, and cannot use commands. Mobile manifestations move in later movement phases and (when armed with melee weapons) can charge and fight (`Aethervoid Pendulum` has no melee weapons and cannot charge or fight). `Banish Manifestation` (Your Hero Phase, 30" range) destroys an enemy manifestation on 2D6 >= `banishment` (7+); a destroyed/banished manifestation cannot be re-summoned that turn.                                                                                                                                        |
| I25 | Strike order                    | Eligible fighters are grouped into `STRIKE-FIRST`, normal, and `STRIKE-LAST` tiers. Having both `STRIKE-FIRST` and `STRIKE-LAST` cancels out to normal. Entering each tier resets the first pick to the active player; within a tier picks alternate.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| I26 | Remaining commands              | `Forward to Victory` (1 CP, your charge phase) re-rolls a charge roll. `Redeploy` (1 CP, enemy movement phase) moves an unengaged unit that has not moved up to D6". `Covering Fire` (1 CP, enemy shooting phase) shoots the closest enemy unit with -1 to hit. `Counter-charge` (2 CP, enemy charge phase) declares a charge with an unengaged unit. `Magical Intervention` (1 CP, enemy hero phase) lets a Wizard or Priest cast or chant once at -1 to the roll. `Power Through` (1 CP, end of turn) lets a unit that charged inflict D3 mortal damage on an engaged enemy with lower Health and move up to its Move characteristic ending > 3" from all enemies.                                                                                                                                             |
| I27 | Sylvaneth lores                 | _Jade Thorns_ (CV 6, `UNLIMITED`): +1 wound on melee attacks (including Companion) for the turn. _Zephyrspite Rush_ (CV 6): run and charge for the turn. _Grasping Roots_ (CV 5): target cannot run and has -1 to charge rolls until start of next turn. _Song Of The Lost_ (CV 4, `UNLIMITED`): -1 to wound for attacks targeting the unit until start of next turn, and on 8+ heals up to 3 or (for non-Monsters) ignores save modifiers (including Rend). _Song Of Dread_ (CV 4, 2nd target on 8+): D3 mortal damage after a Move ability ending within creeping overgrowth (6" of a friendly Wyldwood or overgrown terrain). _Song Of War_ (CV 5, 2nd target on 10+): +1 Rend on melee weapons until start of next turn.                                                                                     |
| I28 | Seraphon lore                   | _Comet's Call_ (CV 7): 1D6 per living model in an enemy unit within 18", 1 mortal damage per 5+. _Mystical Unforging_ (CV 6, `UNLIMITED`): -1 Rend on target enemy's weapons until start of next turn (clamped at 0). _Speed of Huanchi_ (CV 6): target friendly Seraphon unit can run and still shoot and/or charge later in the turn.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| I29 | Warscroll spells                | Each wizard knows any `spell` ability on its own warscroll in addition to its army's lores. _Metamorphosis_ (Alarielle, CV 7, 12"): 2D3 mortal damage on target; if destroyed, gives the closest non-overgrown terrain feature an overgrown token, or inflicts D3 mortal damage on each enemy unit within 6" of the target if all terrain features are already overgrown. _Enrage The Spites_ (Drycha, CV 7): picks up to 3 enemy units within 12" or in creeping overgrowth and inflicts D3 mortal damage on each. _Celestial Deliverance_ (Lord Kroak, CV 7, 18", castable multiple times per phase by Kroak): picks up to 3 different enemy units within 18" and inflicts D3 mortal damage on each. _Celestial Doom_ (Skink Starseer, CV 7, 18"): disables ward rolls on the target for the rest of the turn. |
| I30 | Deployment                      | Alternating deployment (Core Rules 10.0, GHB 2026-27): faction terrain is set up first (attacker then defender wholly within own territory, >= 3" from enemy territory, objectives, and other terrain). Regiments alternate (attacker first, 1 regiment at a time, units sorted largest model count first). Units deploy wholly within own territory and >= 9" from enemy territory. Fast setup (`deployArmies`) remains supported for simulations; full interactive alternating deployment is supported via `newGame(seed, { deploy: true })` and `npcDeployPlacement`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| I31 | Terrain traits                  | Terrain features carry universal traits: `cover`, `obscuring`, `unstable`, `placeOfPower`. Ruins provide cover and are unstable (cannot end moves on them). Outcrops provide cover and are obscuring. Spires are Places of Power and are obscuring. Obscuring terrain blocks visibility to/from non-MONSTER/WAR MACHINE/FLY models wholly within 1" or whose line of sight crosses the footprint (unless attacker is within 3" combat range of target); obscured units have ranged weapon effective range halved. Cover subtracts 1 from hit rolls for non-FLY units that did not charge.                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| I32 | Guarded Hero                    | A HERO without MONSTER or WAR MACHINE and Health <= 10 within 3" of a friendly non-HERO non-manifestation unit with 3+ living models has Guarded Hero (-1 to hit from shooting attacks). If the hero has INFANTRY, enemy models cannot target it with shooting attacks beyond 12".                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| I33 | Model path blocking             | Non-FLY models moving (normal, run, retreat, charge, pile-in, power through, redeploy) cannot pass across any model's base (friend or enemy).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| I34 | Fury season rules               | GHB 2026-27 "Scourge of Aqshy" Fury & Rage rules: Raising the Heat sets attacker starting fury to 1, defender to 2. Simmering Rage (mandatory round start) grants rage dice equal to fury level (0..7). Eruption of Fury (once per turn per army, end of turn) allows an engaged unit to spend up to 3 rage dice to attack in melee without critical hits; unmodified 6 deals D3 mortal damage to each engaged enemy unit and grants +1 opponent fury (max 7); unmodified 1 deals D3 mortal damage to user (no ward). Fight Through the Pain (passive) spends rage dice to lower fury by 1 and negate damage on 3+.                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| I35 | Into the Fire twists & tactics  | Secure the Gateway: friendly units have Anti-charge (+1 Rend) while contesting a controlled objective and player controls the Place of Power in their territory (`spire-w` for player 0, `spire-e` for player 1). Determined Defenders: friendly units add 3 to control scores while the player is the underdog and the unit is wholly outside enemy territory. Battle Tactics: 4 cards in play with 5 VP per tier (Affray -> Strike -> Domination); cannot score on a turn where the player seized initiative.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| I36 | Place of Power boons            | Start of turn timing (Core Rules / GHB 2026-27 Into the Fire). An eligible hero within 3" of a controlled Place of Power may channel it. Rolls 1D6: on 1 suffers D3 mortal damage backfire (warded); on 2+ gains chosen boon: `ignite_fury` (+2 rage dice, +2 fury level), `channelled_wrath` (+1 to casting and chanting rolls this turn), or `dizzying_rage` (can unbind or banish once this turn). Player can also pass.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| I37 | Sylvaneth faction battle traits | Creeping Overgrowth aura range: 0-2 tokens = 6", 3-5 tokens = 9", 6-8 tokens = 12", 9+ tokens = whole battlefield. Awakened Wyldwood Ever Growing rolls 1D6 per wyldwood at round start (3+ adds token to nearest non-overgrown terrain within 12"). Creeping Dread increases enemy command cost by +1 CP if unit is outside 12" of any enemy hero and within Creeping Overgrowth. Endless Growth at end of turn heals D3 damage on friendly Sylvaneth units in Creeping Overgrowth (flat 3 damage on Monsters). Alarielle Seasons of War defaults to Burgeoning (ward 5+ in Overgrowth).                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
