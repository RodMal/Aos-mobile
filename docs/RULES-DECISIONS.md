# Rules decisions

Where the engine had to interpret or simplify a rule, it is written down here so the owner can overrule it. Newest entries go at the bottom of each section.

## Simplifications (to be removed)

| #   | Area                  | What the engine does now                                                                                                                                                                           | Planned                                     |
| --- | --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| S6  | Targets per weapon    | All of a weapon's attacks go to one target unit; attacks of one weapon cannot be split between targets.                                                                                            | When the UI allocates per model.            |
| S7  | Automatic choices     | The engine picks the critical-hit ability of a weapon with several (best expected damage), spends rally points (models first, then healing) and sets up returned models.                           | Player choice if the owner wants it.        |
| S8  | Weapon choices        | Every model attacks with every weapon on its warscroll unless the army list entry gives a loadout (`weapons`). The placeholder Seraphon list has none, so its Kroxigor swing both hammer and maul. | Loadouts on every list entry with a choice. |
| S9  | Incoherent casualties | Casualties are picked to keep the unit coherent. If no choice does, extra models are removed at once until it is coherent (the rule does this at the end of the turn).                             | Revisit if it ever matters in play.         |
| S13 | Manifestation parts   | Summoned manifestations use their single resolved warscroll footprint and statline; warscroll passive/activated abilities on manifestations come in M4.                                            | M4.                                         |
